ABSTRACT: How has China's strategic narrative around nuclear weapons shifted over time? Far more than a marker of military strength, the bomb has doubled as a symbol tied to regime legitimacy, international prestige, and nationalism. Taking this symbolic dimension seriously, this chapter draws on nuclear-related coverage in the People's Daily from 1946 to 2023, pairing quantitative text mining — tracking article frequency and keyword patterns — with qualitative content analysis of shifting tone and discursive themes. Together, these methods trace how the image of the bomb was built and rebuilt across successive eras, and lay bare the strategic logic behind the discourse China has produced around its nuclear arsenal.
